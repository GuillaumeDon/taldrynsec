Why I Created TaldrynSec

The first post on this blog — the story behind this website.

Hey! If you're reading this, you've stumbled upon TaldrynSec — my little corner of the internet dedicated to cybersecurity. Let me explain why this site exists.

The problem: learning without documenting

When I started training in cybersecurity on TryHackMe, I took notes everywhere: Obsidian, Notion, random text files scattered across my machine... The problem? I never came back to them. Notes kept piling up without ever serving as a real reference.

I realized that the best way to retain something is to rephrase it, structure it, and make it accessible — not just for myself, but for anyone.

The idea: a second brain

TaldrynSec is my second brain. Each revision sheet is a condensed version of what I've learned, structured for quick review. Each blog post is a deeper reflection on a topic I find interesting.

"If you can't explain it simply, you don't understand it well enough."

What you'll find here

  • Revision sheets — concise, visual, with built-in quizzes
  • Blog posts — lessons learned, discoveries, thoughts
  • My journey — certifications, projects, progress

What's next

This site will grow with me. I'll keep updating it as I learn. If you're also learning cybersecurity, I hope these resources help you out.

Feel free to reach out if you want to chat. We learn better together.

— Taldryn
